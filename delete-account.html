IR Planner

Delete Your Account

You can delete your IR Planner account and its associated data at any time, either directly in the app or by emailing us.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open IR Planner and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email ziad@missiry.com from the address associated with your account with the subject “IR Planner account deletion.” We will verify the request and delete the account within two business days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email, name, sign-in identities)
  • Your credit balance and subscription status
  • Your purchase history records held by IR Planner

Deletion removes all account data; no account data is retained after deletion. Records of payments processed by Apple or Google remain with those platforms and are governed by their terms — IR Planner cannot delete them. IR Planner never collects or stores patient data, and sizing inputs are never saved to your account.
