IR Planner

Delete Your Account

You can delete your IR Planner account and its associated data at any time, either directly in the app or by emailing us.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open IR Planner and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email contact@zdigitech.co.uk from the address associated with your account with the subject “IR Planner account deletion.” We will verify the request and delete the account within two business days.

What is deleted straight away

  • Your account (name, email address, password hash and Apple or Google sign-in link)
  • Your credit balance and subscription status held by IR Planner
  • Your in-app purchase history held by IR Planner

What is kept, and for how long

  • Database backups may still contain your account for up to 7 days. After that it no longer exists in any backup.
  • Sign-in, security and email-delivery logs (which can include your email address and IP address) are kept only for the short period our providers keep them, then deleted automatically.
  • RevenueCat, which checks purchases for us, keeps its record of your store purchases. Email us if you want us to ask RevenueCat to delete it.
  • Apple and Google keep their own records of payments and subscriptions under their own terms. We cannot delete them.

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel it first in your Apple Account subscriptions or in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. IR Planner never collects or stores patient data, and sizing inputs are never saved to your account.
