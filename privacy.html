IR Planner

Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026 · replaces the version of 6 July 2026

IR Planner is a reference app for medical professionals that checks endovascular device compatibility and calculates EVAR stent-graft sizing. This policy explains what personal data the app uses, why, who else handles it and what rights you have.

1. Who is responsible for your data

IR Planner is provided by ZDIGITECH LTD, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17504661), 71-75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London, WC2H 9JQ, United Kingdom. ZDIGITECH LTD is the controller of the personal data described here and is registered with the Information Commission (ICO) under registration number ZC269998. We have not appointed a data protection officer; questions and requests go to our director at contact@zdigitech.co.uk.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data. Your name and email address, and either a password (stored only as a secure hash) or the identifier given by Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real email.
  • Credit and purchase data. Your credit balance, your purchase history in the app (product, number of credits, date and time) and whether you have an IR Planner Pro subscription. RevenueCat links your purchases to your account using an app user ID.
  • Emails we send. Sign-up confirmation and password-reset codes are sent to your email address through our email provider.
  • Technical data. Our backend keeps short-lived sign-in and security logs, which can include your IP address, device type and the time of each sign-in.

3. Data we do not collect

  • No patient data. The measurements you enter for EVAR sizing are processed on your device. They are not sent to us or stored with your account; only the use of a credit is recorded.
  • No advertising identifiers, ad networks or third-party analytics.
  • No location, contacts, photos or health records.

4. Why we use your data and our lawful basis

  • Running your account, credits and subscription, and sending sign-up and password-reset emails: necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • Keeping the service secure and preventing abuse (sign-in logs): our legitimate interest in a service that works and is protected.
  • Keeping business, tax and accounting records, and dealing with complaints and claims: our legal obligations, and our legitimate interest in defending our position.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or send you marketing.

5. Who else handles your data

  • Supabase: sign-in and database hosting. Your account and credit data are stored in Supabase's London (UK) region.
  • RevenueCat (United States): checks purchases and manages subscription status.
  • Resend (United States): sends sign-up and password-reset emails.
  • Apple and Google: take payment for purchases made through the App Store or Google Play, and run Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In if you choose them. They act under their own privacy policies.

If we sell or transfer the business, or part of it, your data may pass to the buyer, who must use it in the way this policy describes.

6. Transfers outside the UK

Some of these providers are based in, or support their services from, the United States. Where a provider is certified under the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (the "UK-US data bridge"), we rely on that certification. Where it is not, we rely on the provider's data processing terms incorporating the ICO's International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, which commit the provider to protect your data to UK standards.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Account, credit and in-app purchase data: while your account exists. They are deleted when you delete your account.
  • Database backups: up to 7 days, after which deleted data no longer exists in any backup.
  • Sign-in, security and email-delivery logs: only for the short period our providers keep them, then deleted automatically.
  • Records of store purchases held by RevenueCat, Apple and Google: kept by them for their own accounting and legal purposes. We can ask RevenueCat to delete its record on request.

See Delete your account for exactly what is deleted and what is kept.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete Account. If you can no longer use the app, email contact@zdigitech.co.uk from the address on your account. Deleting the app does not delete your account, and does not cancel a subscription.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, passwords are stored only as hashes, and each account can read only its own records. No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will tell you without undue delay and report it to the ICO within 72 hours as the law requires.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to restrict how we use it, to receive it in a portable format, and to object to uses based on our legitimate interests. To use any of these rights, email contact@zdigitech.co.uk. We do not charge a fee unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive, we may ask you to confirm your identity, and we will reply within one month (or tell you within that month if a complex request needs up to two months more).

11. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please contact us first. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days, look into it and tell you the outcome. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commission (ICO) at ico.org.uk, by telephone on 0303 123 1113, or by post to Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 5AF.

12. Children

IR Planner is for qualified healthcare professionals. It is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

13. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
