Privacy Policy

Effective 6 July 2026

IR Planner is a reference application for medical professionals that checks endovascular device compatibility and computes EVAR stent-graft sizing. This policy describes what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data we collect

  • Account data. Your email address (or, if you use Sign in with Apple, the identifier Apple provides — which may be a private relay address) and a password hash. Stored with our backend provider, Supabase.
  • Purchase and credit data. Your credit balance, purchase history (product identifiers, credit amounts, timestamps) and subscription status. Purchases are processed by Apple; receipt validation and subscription status are handled by RevenueCat. We never see your payment card details.

Data we do not collect

  • No patient data. Measurements you enter for sizing calculations are processed on your device and are not transmitted or stored by us.
  • No advertising identifiers, no ad networks, no third-party analytics.
  • No location data, contacts, photos, or health records.

How data is used

Account and purchase data are used solely to operate the service: authenticating you, maintaining your credit balance, and honoring your subscription. Data is never sold or shared for marketing.

Service providers

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting.
  • RevenueCat — purchase validation and subscription management.
  • Apple — payment processing for all in-app purchases.

Data retention and deletion

Your account data is kept while your account exists. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time in the app: Credits & Account → Delete Account. You can also request deletion by emailing ziad@missiry.com.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
