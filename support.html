IR Planner

Support

IR Planner is a reference tool for medical professionals: it checks endovascular device compatibility and computes EVAR stent-graft sizing against manufacturer IFU criteria.

Contact

For any question, bug report, or account request, email contact@zdigitech.co.uk. We aim to reply within two business days.

Common questions

How do credits work? New accounts start with 10 free credits. Each EVAR sizing calculation uses 1 credit. Compatibility checking is always free. You can buy more credits or subscribe to IR Planner Pro for unlimited sizing.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription? On iPhone, go to Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions. On Android, open the Google Play Store app, tap your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → IR Planner → Cancel subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund? For an App Store purchase, ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com; Apple decides all App Store refunds. For a Google Play purchase, ask Google Play within 48 hours of buying (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history). After 48 hours, email us with your Google Play order number.

How do I restore a purchase? Sign in with the same account, open Profile in the app and tap “Restore Purchases.” Purchases restore within the same store (App Store or Google Play).

How do I delete my account? Open Profile in the app and tap “Delete Account,” or email us to request deletion. See Delete your account for what is deleted and what is kept.

Important

IR Planner is a reference aid, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice. Always verify device compatibility and sizing against the manufacturer’s current Instructions for Use and your own clinical judgment. See our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
