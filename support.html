Support

IR Planner is a reference tool for medical professionals: it checks endovascular device compatibility and computes EVAR stent-graft sizing against manufacturer IFU criteria.

Contact

For any question, bug report, or account request, email ziad@missiry.com. We aim to reply within two business days.

Common questions

How do credits work? New accounts start with 10 free credits. Each EVAR sizing calculation uses 1 credit. Compatibility checking is always free. You can buy more credits or subscribe to IR Planner Pro for unlimited sizing.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription? On your device, go to Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions.

How do I restore a purchase? Open Profile in the app and tap “Restore Purchases.”

How do I delete my account? Open Profile in the app and tap “Delete Account,” or email us to request deletion.

Important

IR Planner is a reference aid, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice. Always verify device compatibility and sizing against the manufacturer’s current Instructions for Use and your own clinical judgment. See our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
